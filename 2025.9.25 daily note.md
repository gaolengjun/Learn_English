2025.9.25 daily note
It was the Mid-Autumn Festival yesterday, and we had two days off. I cycled around the city. I especially like cycling along tree-lined paths, as there are trees on both sides of the street that can block the strong sunshine for me.
Then I rode to the river bank. The river breeze made me feel comfortable. Many people were relaxing there or working out. The sun was setting, and the sunlight grew dim. Its color turned orange, like an orange hanging in the sky.
There were a lot of people in the park. They were sitting or lying on the grass. A lady was singing a song. All the people were enjoying the afternoon.
When evening came, I took the subway back home. I was in no hurry to go inside, so I walked around my neighborhood. I went to a café and sat down.
Then I saw a pretty girl in a yellow traditional costume getting ready to perform outside. I walked out of the café. She took out a xylophone, then sat in front of the microphone to play. Many audience members gathered around her, taking photos with their phones.
There were so many people that I couldn't see the girl. I felt a little disappointed and went back home.
 
错误说明
 
节日表达：Moon Festival 规范常用表达为 Mid-Autumn Festival，节日前需加定冠词the；two day off 改为 two days off（可数名词复数）。 
动词与拼写错误：bicycled to around cities 删去多余的to，改为cycled around the city；clcying 为拼写错误，改为cycling；tree 改为复数trees（街道两侧是多棵树）。 
时态与主谓一致：全文描述过去发生的事，需统一使用一般过去时，are 改为were，was搭配复数主语时改为were（如They was 改为They were）。 
冠词与固定搭配：a orange 改为 an orange（orange以元音音素开头），hung on the sky 改为 hanging in the sky（空中的固定搭配用in the sky）；return my place 改为 go back home / return to my place，entree 为拼写错误改为enter。 
细节错误：sited 为拼写错误，改为sat；than 为形近词错误，改为then；taking phones of her with phone 改为 taking photos of her with their phones，符合语境逻辑。
